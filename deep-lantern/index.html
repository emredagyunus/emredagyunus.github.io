FAQ

I bought something but it is gone. How do I get it back?

Purchases made with real money (Remove Ads, Commander Premium, Lantern+) belong to your Apple ID or Google account. Open the Store in the game, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought them with. Pearls spent in the game are not purchases and cannot be restored.

How do I cancel Lantern+?

Lantern+ is a monthly subscription managed by the store. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period so it does not renew.

How do I delete my data?

In the game open Settings → Support → Delete my data and confirm. This deletes your progress, pearls, characters bought with pearls, your diver name and your dive history, on your device and on our server, and the game starts fresh. Purchases made with real money stay with your Apple or Google account and come back with Restore purchases. You can also email us from the address above and we delete it for you. See the privacy policy for details.

Notifications: what does the game send, and how do I turn them off?

All notifications are scheduled on your phone (no push server): at most one a day, never during your quiet hours, and only after you allowed them. Change them in Settings → Notifications in the game, or turn them off for Deep Lantern in your phone's settings.

There is no sound on my iPhone.

The game follows the iPhone's silent switch (the ring / silent switch or Action button): when the phone is on silent, the game is muted too. Switch silent mode off and check the sound and music sliders in Settings → Audio & feedback, and the quick mute buttons on the pause screen.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game, your progress and your pearls are saved on your phone. Ads, purchases and restoring them need a connection.
