Deep Lantern is a typing game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You pilot an anglerfish submarine into the deep sea and stop the creatures coming at you by typing the words they carry: modes for speed, focus, learning a new language and relaxing, in 15 typing languages.

Contact us

Questions, bugs, purchases, ideas — write to us and we answer as soon as we can:

deeplanterngame@gmail.com

In the game: SETTINGS → SUPPORT → Contact us. Please add your Support code (copy it from the same section), your phone model and what you were doing.

Questions

I bought something but it is gone. How do I get it back?

Purchases made with real money (Remove Ads, Captain Pack, Lantern+) belong to your Apple ID or Google account. Open STORE in the game, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought them with.

How do I cancel Lantern+?

Lantern+ is a monthly or yearly subscription managed by the store. On iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period so it does not renew; it stays active until that end.

Is my progress saved if I reinstall the game?

Yes. Your progress, pearls and characters are saved on your phone and backed up to an anonymous account on our server, so reinstalling on the same phone brings them back. Moving them to a new phone isn't supported yet; purchases made with real money come back on any phone with Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

In the game open SETTINGS → SUPPORT → Delete my data and confirm. This deletes your account on our server and everything on your phone — progress, pearls, characters bought with pearls, diver name and dive history — and the game starts fresh. Purchases made with real money stay with your Apple or Google account and come back with Restore purchases. You can also email us and we delete it for you. See the privacy policy for details.

Notifications: what does the game send, and how do I turn them off?

All notifications are scheduled on your phone (there is no push server): at most one a day, never during your quiet hours, and only after you allowed them. Change them in SETTINGS → NOTIFICATIONS in the game, or turn them off for Deep Lantern in your phone's settings.

There is no sound.

The game plays sound even when the phone is on silent, so check the game's own controls: the sound and music sliders in SETTINGS → AUDIO & FEEDBACK, and the quick mute buttons on the pause screen. Then check the phone's volume.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every mode can be played offline; pearls and progress you earn are sent to our server once you are back online. The leaderboards, ads and purchases need a connection.
