This policy explains what the Deep Lantern game for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the app", "we") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you can delete it. Contact: deeplanterngame@gmail.com.

In short

There is no sign-up and no login: we never ask for your email, real name or a password. A random device id gives the game an anonymous account on our server, which backs up your progress, keeps your pearls and characters safe and runs the leaderboards. Crash reports, game analytics, ads and purchases go through the services listed below. We never record what you type. You can delete everything from inside the game (SETTINGS → SUPPORT → Delete my data).

What we collect and why

Anonymous account (device id)

On first launch the app creates a random device identifier (on iOS the identifier for vendor; on Android the Android ID, or a random UUID) and keeps it in the phone's secure storage. It is sent to our server (hosted on Supabase), which stores only a keyed hash of it, never the id itself, and links it to an anonymous user id. With it we also store basic device information: platform, OS version, device model and brand, app version and build, language, screen size and whether it is a real device or a simulator. The session that keeps you signed in is stored in the phone's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore).
Why: to give your install an account without asking for personal details.

Your game: progress backup, pearls and purchases

Your progress — records, statistics and badges, campaign stars, quest progress, tutorial steps, your own word lists, your Polyglot review cards and your last 50 dives — is saved on your phone and backed up to our server. Your pearls (balance and the history of what you earned and spent), characters, abilities and what your purchases unlock are kept on our server, and the daily rewards, quests and gifts you claim are recorded there.
Why: so a reinstall brings everything back, and so pearls and items can't be lost or forged.

Dives

When a dive ends, a summary of it is sent to our server: the mode and its settings, score, depth, duration, letters typed and missed, typing speed, bonuses, abilities used, whether a second chance was taken, and a timing profile of the dive — how many gaps between key presses fell into each time range, and the totals of each zone. It never contains which keys you pressed or what you typed.
Why: to pay a dive's pearls, keep the leaderboards fair and spot cheating.

Diver name, profile picture, country and leaderboards

The diver name you choose, the submarine you pick as your profile picture and a country — your phone's region, or the one you pick on the leaderboard — are sent to our server and shown to other players next to your best scores on the leaderboards. Names pass a word filter. Don't use your real name if you don't want it to be seen. We also store your time zone's offset from UTC (not your location), so the game knows when your day starts for daily rewards and streaks.

Feedback and reports

If you write a note in the rating sheet, its stars and text are sent to us. If you report another player on a leaderboard, their anonymous id and the reason are sent to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to answer you.

Device check at sign-up

When a new account is created, the app asks Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity to confirm that it is a genuine copy of the game on a real device; our server stores the result with your device record. A one-way hash of the network address the sign-up came from is kept for a short time to limit how many accounts one network can create.
Why: to stop fake accounts from farming rewards and flooding the leaderboards.

Crash reports and performance (Sentry)

When the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry: the error and stack trace, device model, OS and app version, the screen you were on, a short trail of recent in-app actions (for example "dive started", "store opened") and the anonymous user id. A small sample of screen transitions is measured for performance. We do not send your IP address as personal data, and no names or emails are attached.
Why: to find and fix bugs.

Usage events

The app records what happens in the game — screens opened, dives started and finished with their mode and score, purchases started, rewards claimed, settings changed — with the anonymous user id. They travel as the "trail" inside Sentry crash reports and contain ids and numbers, not personal details.
Why: to understand which parts of the game work and to fix problems.

Game analytics (GameAnalytics)

A summary of how the game is played is sent to GameAnalytics: dives started, completed or failed (mode, campaign level, depth reached, score), pearls earned and spent, purchases (product, price and currency), ads watched or unavailable, tutorial steps, the rating prompt and settings changes. GameAnalytics also collects device and usage data (device model, OS and app version, language and country, session start and length) and a device identifier: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) only when it is available to apps — on iPhone only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt — otherwise an app-specific id (the identifier for vendor on iOS, the app set id on Android). Nothing is sent before the ad consent step. Where the GDPR applies (EEA, UK, Switzerland) GameAnalytics only starts if, in Google's consent form, you consent to storing and accessing information on your device, to measuring content performance and to GameAnalytics as a vendor; if you withdraw that consent in SETTINGS → Ad privacy choices, sending stops. It never receives your diver name, our anonymous user id, an email or a real name, and no events are sent per key press.
Why: to see where dives get too hard, which rewards and offers players use, and to balance the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows rewarded videos (only when you choose to watch one) and short full-screen ads between dives — never banners, never during a dive and never in the CADET mode for young players. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) when allowed, IP address, device information and how you interact with the ads. On iPhone the app first explains and then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you do not allow tracking, AdMob does not get the IDFA and shows non-personalised ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time in SETTINGS → Ad privacy choices. When a rewarded video pays a reward, Google confirms it to our server with your anonymous user id. Buying Remove Ads, the Captain Pack or Lantern+ turns the full-screen ads off.
Why: ads keep the game free.

Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat checks your receipts and tells the app and our server what you own, using the anonymous user id. It receives your purchase history (products, dates, prices, store country), the app user id and device information.
Why: to unlock what you bought, credit pearl packs and restore purchases on a new phone.

Notifications

Reminders (for example "your daily reward is ready") are scheduled locally on your phone, only after you allow them. There is no push server and no push token is collected.

Kept only on your phone

Your settings (sound, haptics, languages, difficulty, accessibility), notification preferences and history, ad and rating-prompt counters and your dive history beyond the last 50 dives never leave your phone.

What we do not collect

No email address (unless you email us), phone number, real name, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, account login or what you type in the game. We do not sell personal data.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we process your data to provide the game you asked for (the account, progress backup, pearls, purchases and leaderboards), for our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure and fair and in fixing bugs (dive checks, the device check, crash reports, usage events), and with your consent for personalised ads and game analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before.

How long we keep it

Your account, progress backup, pearls history, characters, scores on the leaderboards, feedback and reports are kept until you delete your data or ask us to. Dive summaries are deleted after 30 days; only the best scores they set stay on the leaderboards. To stop the ranked Daily from being replayed, a one-way hash of your device and whether it used today's Daily attempt are kept for 48 hours at most, also after you delete your data. So that a one-time reward can't be collected again with a new account on the same phone, that device hash is also kept with the name of each one-time reward it already received, without your account or any date: Fener's tour bonus and once-only store offers for as long as the game runs, the first-dive bonus and the daily chest until two days after their day. The device check's result is deleted with your data (a sign-up that was refused is kept 90 days), and the network address hash after 2 days. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. Game analytics data is kept by GameAnalytics under its own policy. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat, Apple and Google as long as the law and their own policies require (for example for tax and refunds). Emails are kept as long as needed to answer them. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

In the game: SETTINGS → SUPPORT → Delete my data. After you confirm, your account and its records are deleted from our server at once, everything stored on your phone (progress, pearls, characters bought with pearls, diver name, dive history, settings) is erased, and the game starts fresh. Purchases made with real money stay with your Apple or Google account and come back with Restore purchases. You can also email deeplanterngame@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Crash reports already sent to Sentry expire on their own within 90 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, LGPD, PIPA, APPI or US state laws) you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Ad consent can be changed in the game (SETTINGS → Ad privacy choices) or in your phone's tracking settings. Write to us for anything else; you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Deep Lantern is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The CADET mode for young players shows no ads at all. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we update this page and its effective date; important changes are also announced in the game.
